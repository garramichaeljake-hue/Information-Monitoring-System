ABOUT THE WEBSITE

The Web-Based School Information Monitoring System is designed to provide a convenient platform for accessing school-related information at Minuyan National High School.

It brings announcements, events, and schedules together so that students and visitors can find important information in one online location.

OBJECTIVES

  • Make school information easier to access.
  • Organize announcements and updates.
  • Support communication and information sharing.
  • Provide a search feature for finding information.
  • Allow approved administrators to manage website content.

HOW TO USE THE WEBSITE

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Select Announcements, Events, or Schedules.
  3. Read the information displayed.
  4. Use the homepage search to find specific information.
