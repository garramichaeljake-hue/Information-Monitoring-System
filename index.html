ABOUT THE SYSTEM

The Web-Based School Information Monitoring System is designed to provide students and other members of the school community with an organized and convenient way to access school information.

Through this website, visitors can view announcements, school events, schedules, and other important updates.

BENEFITS OF THE WEBSITE

  • Easier access to school information.
  • Organized announcements and updates.
  • Convenient viewing of school events and schedules.
  • Search feature for finding information.
  • Administrators can publish updates through the admin page.
